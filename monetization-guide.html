2. Pay-Per-Use Reports via Razorpay (₹49 & ₹99)

The core reconciliation tool is 100% free. Users can optionally pay to unlock enriched management and compliance reports.

Report Option Price What It Contains
Executive Financial Summary ₹49 Financial KPI cards, GL matching percentages, and a prioritized discrepancy action checklist (High/Med/Low priority).
Advanced Mismatch & Root Cause Analysis ₹99 Everything in ₹49 report + Root Cause categorization (timing vs tax vs unmapped lines), GST ITC compliance audit, and Servify Net Settlement matrix.

How Money Reaches Your Bank

  1. Customer clicks ⭐ Premium Reports and selects a report.
  2. Customer pays via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Cards, or Net Banking through the Razorpay modal.
  3. Razorpay receives the funds and automatically deposits them into your bank account within 1–2 business days (T+1 or T+2 settlements).
Connecting Your Bank Account (Razorpay Setup):
  1. Create a free account at https://razorpay.com.
  2. Complete KYC and enter your Bank Account Number & IFSC code.
  3. Copy your Key ID (starts with rzp_live_...) from Razorpay Dashboard > Settings > API Keys.
  4. Go to your Admin Portal > Settings and paste your Key ID (or add RAZORPAY_KEY_ID and RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET in Netlify Environment Variables).

4. Google AdSense (How Ads Work & Pay You)

Google AdSense connects corporate advertisers to your site and automatically serves targeted banner ads.

Where Ads Appear

  • In the website footer slot (above the bottom legal links).
  • In selected secondary recommendation slots.
  • Zero Interference: Ads will never appear inside the Excel upload boxes, calculation progress bars, results tables, or download buttons.

Activating AdSense

  1. Sign up at https://adsense.google.com with your Google account.
  2. Add your website URL: https://reconciliation-automation.netlify.app/.
  3. Copy your Publisher ID (format: ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX).
  4. Open your Admin Portal > Google AdSense tab.
  5. Check "Enable Google AdSense Advertisements", paste your Publisher ID, and click Save.

How Google Pays You

Add your bank account under AdSense > Payments > Payment info. Google automatically transfers your earnings on the 21st of every month via direct bank wire / NEFT once your account reaches the minimum payout threshold ($100 / ~₹1,000).
